- A seed germinates when its environment gives it enough of the right signals and resources to “wake up” and resume growth.

- ==Germination ≠ plant growth.==
	- *Germination specifically refers to the seed **starting development and producing the initial seedling**, commonly marked by the emergence of the radicle/root.*

| Requirement              | What it does                                                       |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Water**                | Activates the seed's internal processes and allows cells to expand |
| **Oxygen**               | Allows respiration → energy production                             |
| **Suitable temperature** | Makes the biochemical reactions work efficiently                   |
| **Viable seed**          | The embryo itself must still be alive/capable of growth            |
|                          |                                                                    |
