# Idea: Balcony Gardening

A small-scale agriculture project designed for people with limited space, such as balconies. The idea is to start growing food or plants using a mini agricultural setup that can fit into a small living space.

## Problem
- Walang healthy soil na available
- Limited space para magtanim
- Kulang sa knowledge/experience sa pagtanim
